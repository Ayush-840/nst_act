## Problem Statement

A bank wants to find customers whose account balance is higher than the average balance of their own branch.
An analyst first attempts to solve the problem using a non-correlated subquery, but realizes that it compares balances against the overall bank average instead of each branch&#39;s average.

The task is to demonstrate this limitation and then correct it using a correlated subquery.

The bank wants customers whose balance is greater than the average balance of their own branch.

 Write a query using a non-correlated subquery and observe why it does not fully solve the requirement.

## Solution

```js
SELECT 
    account_id, 
    customer_name, 
    branch, 
    balance
FROM 
    ACCOUNTS a1
WHERE 
    balance > (
        SELECT AVG(balance) 
        FROM ACCOUNTS a2 
        WHERE a2.branch = a1.branch
    );
```
