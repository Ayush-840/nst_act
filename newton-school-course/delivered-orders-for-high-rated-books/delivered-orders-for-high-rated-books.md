## Problem Statement

A publishing company maintains three tables in its database to manage book sales and deliveries:

 
 book_info: stores information about each book, including its ID, title, and rating.

 

 
 orders: keeps track of book orders, including which book was ordered and the date of the order.

 

 
 deliveries: records the delivery details for each order, including the delivery status.

 

The company&rsquo;s analytics team wants to generate a report to find out which highly rated books (those with rating above or equals to 4.0) have been successfully delivered to customers.

Write a SQL query to retrieve the following details:

 
 The book title

 

 
 The rating of the book

 

 
 The order date

 

 
 The delivery status

 

The report should only include books with a rating greater than 4.0 and with a delivery status marked as &#39;Delivered&#39;.

## Solution

```js
-- Write your code here
select
b.book_title,
b.book_rating,
o.order_date,
d.delivery_status
from book_info b
join orders o on b.book_id = o.book_id
join deliveries d on o.order_id = d.order_id
where b.book_rating >= 4.0 and d.delivery_status='Delivered'
```
