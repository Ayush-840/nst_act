## Problem Statement

A university is preparing the list of students eligible for a merit scholarship.

The university has decided that a student is eligible if:

 Their score is 80 or above, AND

 They belong to either the Computer Science or Information Technology department, AND

 They are NOT marked as suspended.

Write an SQL query to display the names of all eligible students along with their department and score.

## Solution

```py
// ─── 1 ───
select student_name,department,score from students
where score >= 80 AND (department='Computer Science' or department='Information Technology') And is_suspended=False
```
