Newton Bank runs a customer referral programme. Existing customers can refer new customers to the bank. The marketing team wants a report showing each referred customer along with the name of the customer who referred them.

(3 rows)

## Solution

```js
SELECT 
    c.customer_name AS customer_name,
    r.customer_name AS referrer_name
FROM 
    customers c
JOIN 
    customers r 
ON 
    c.referred_by = r.customer_id;
```
