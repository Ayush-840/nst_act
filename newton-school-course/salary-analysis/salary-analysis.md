For each department, calculate:

 Total number of employees

 Average salary rounded to 2 decimal places

 Maximum salary

 Minimum salary

 At least 3 employees

 Average salary is greater than 60000

Sort the result by average salary in descending order.

department

select department,
count(*) as employee_count, 
round(avg(salary),2) as average_salary,
max(salary) as maximum_salary ,
min(salary) as minimum_salary 
from employees 
group by department
having count(*)>=3 and round(avg(salary),2)>60000
order by round(avg(salary),2) desc;
