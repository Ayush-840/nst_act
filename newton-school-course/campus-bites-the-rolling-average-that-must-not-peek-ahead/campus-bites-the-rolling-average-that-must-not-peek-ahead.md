## Problem Statement

Campus Bites runs three separate outlets on campus. Their live operations dashboard shows, for every day, a smoothed revenue figure per outlet. Today&#39;s revenue is blended with the two days immediately before it, so that a single unusually good or bad day does not cause a sudden spike in the graph.

Since this is a live dashboard, the smoothing can only look backward at days that have already happened. It must never include future days. On an outlet&#39;s first or second day of data, when three days of history are not yet available, the smoothed figure should use however many days are available so far.

Task:

 * For each outlet and date, compute rolling_avg_revenue: the average revenue across the current day and up to the 2 days immediately before it, computed separately for each outlet.

 * Round rolling_avg_revenue to 2 decimal places.

 * Order the results by outlet, then chronologically within each outlet.

## Solution

```js
select 
outlet,
sale_date,
revenue,
round(
    avg(revenue) over(
        partition by outlet
        order by sale_date
        rows between 2 PRECEDING and current row 
    ),2
)as rolling_avg_revenue
from outlet_sales
order by
outlet,
sale_date;
```
