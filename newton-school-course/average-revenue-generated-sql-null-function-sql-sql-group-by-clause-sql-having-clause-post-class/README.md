## Problem Statement

Imagine you are managing a database for a bookstore. The table book_sales contains information about sales transactions. Each row represents a sale, including the book&#39;s title, the quantity sold, the sale date, and the total revenue generated. Your task is to write a query to display,book_title, total quantity sold as "total_quantity_sold"

and average revenue per book as "average_revenue_per_book",

average_revenue_per_book = ROUND(SUM(total_revenue) / SUM(quantity_sold), 2) 

retrieve the books that have a total quantity sold greater than 50 and an average revenue per book greater than $15.

The average Revenue generated should be rounded to two decimal places.

Output the results in descending order of average revenue per book.

&lt;schema&gt;[{"name":"book_sales", "columns":[{"name":"sale_id", "type":"INT", "primary_key":true}, {"name":"book_title", "type":"VARCHAR(255)"}, {"name":"quantity_sold", "type":"INT"}, {"name":"sale_date", "type":"DATE"}, {"name":"total_revenue", "type":"INT"}]}]&lt;/schema&gt;

 

### Input
No input required

### Output
Order of output columns

book_title
total_quantity_sold
average_revenue_per_book

### Example
Sample Input:

+---------+-----------------------------+---------------+------------+---------------+
| sale_id | book_title | quantity_sold | sale_date | total_revenue |
+---------+-----------------------------+---------------+------------+---------------+
| 1 | The Great Gatsby | 25 | 2023-01-15 | 350 |
| 2 | To Kill a Mockingbird | 35 | 2023-02-02 | 600 |
| 3 | 1984 | 20 | 2023-02-10 | 200 |
| 4 | Bestseller | 55 | 2023-03-05 | 850 |

+---------+-----------------------------+---------------+------------+---------------+

Sample Output:

+---------------------+--------------------+-------------------------+
| book_title | total_quantity_sold | average_revenue_per_book |
+---------------------+--------------------+-------------------------+
| Blockbuster Series | 90 | 30.00 |
| Top Revenue Book | 80 | 27.50 |
| Bestseller | 55 | 15.45 |
+---------------------+--------------------+-------------------------+

Explanation:
The Great Gatsby, To Kill a Mockingbird, and 1984 do not qualify as they fail the criteria of total quantity sold &gt; 50 or average revenue &gt; 15.

- Blockbuster Series: Sold 90 copies, Average revenue = 2700/90=30.00
- Top Revenue Book: Sold 80 copies, Average revenue = 2200/80=27.50
- Bestseller: Sold 55 copies, Average revenue = 850/55=15.45
- Only Blockbuster Series, Top Revenue Book and Bestseller meet the criteria and are sorted by descending average revenue.

## Solution

```js
select 
book_title, sum(quantity_sold) as total_quantity_sold, 
round(sum(total_revenue) / sum(quantity_sold), 2) as average_revenue_per_book
from book_sales 
group by book_title 
having total_quantity_sold > 50 and average_revenue_per_book > 15
order by average_revenue_per_book desc;
```
