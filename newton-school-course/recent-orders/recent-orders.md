## Problem Statement

You are given an \`orders\` table:

+----------+-------------+------------+--------+
| order_id | customer_name | order_date | amount |
+----------+-------------+------------+--------+
| 1 | Aarav | 2025-07-10 | 1200 |
| 2 | Priya | 2025-06-28 | 850 |
| 3 | Rahul | 2025-07-18 | 2100 |
+----------+-------------+------------+--------+

Return the \`order_id\`, \`customer_name\`, and \`order_date\` of all orders placed in the last **30 days from July 20, 2025**.

Order the result by \`order_date\` in descending order.

### Output
 order_id | customer_name | order_date 
----------+---------------+------------
 3 | Rahul | 2025-07-18
 1 | Aarav | 2025-07-10
 2 | Priya | 2025-06-28
(3 rows)

## Solution

```js
select 
order_id,
customer_name,
order_date 
from orders
where order_date between '2025-06-20' and '2025-07-20'
order by order_date desc;
```
