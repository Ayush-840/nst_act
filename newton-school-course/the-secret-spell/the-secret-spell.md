## Problem Statement

The inscription revealed that the spell had been intentionally written backward to protect its power.

To help Avinash unlock the hidden magic, you must take the given spell and reverse it to restore its true form.

### Output

Explanation
The given spell "live on time" is reversed character-by-character to reveal its true form. 
When reversed, each character (including spaces) flips position, forming the phrase "emit no evil".
