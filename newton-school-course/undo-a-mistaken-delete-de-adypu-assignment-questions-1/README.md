## Problem Statement

You are given an accounts table (acc_id, holder, balance). Inside a transaction, delete every account, then realise it was a mistake and ROLLBACK the whole transaction. Return all accounts (acc_id, holder, balance) .

The accounts table initially contains:

+--------+--------+---------+
| acc_id | holder | balance |
+--------+--------+---------+
| 1 | Aarav | 3000 |
| 2 | Priya | 1000 |
+--------+--------+---------+

### Output
BEGIN
DELETE 2
ROLLBACK
 acc_id | holder | balance 
--------+--------+---------
 1 | Aarav | 3000
 2 | Priya | 1000
(2 rows)

## Solution

```js
begin;
Delete from accounts;
ROLLBACK;
select acc_id, holder, balance from accounts
```
