Your Task:

Write a SQL query that:

 * Displays full_name, blood_group, and registration_date

 * Combines first_name and last_name into a single column called full_name (using CONCAT)

 * Filters patients who registered in the year 2025 and whose blood_group is A-

 * Presents the records in reverse chronological order based on registration_date

----------------+-------------+-------------------
Aman Joshi | A- | 2025-07-21
Kunal Deshmukh | A- | 2025-01-30
(2 rows)

## Solution

```js
select 
concat(first_name,' ',last_name) as full_name,
blood_group,registration_date
from patients
where 
extract(year from registration_date)=2025 and
blood_group='A-'
order by registration_date desc
```
