The analytics team at Newton Bank wants to identify customers whose account balance is above the average balance of their branch.
Management wants to verify whether both approaches produce the same result and evaluate which approach is easier to understand and maintain.

## Solution

```js
SELECT 
    account_id,
    customer_name,
    branch,
    balance
FROM 
    ACCOUNTS a1
WHERE 
    balance > (
        SELECT AVG(balance)
        FROM ACCOUNTS a2
        WHERE a2.branch = a1.branch
    );
```
