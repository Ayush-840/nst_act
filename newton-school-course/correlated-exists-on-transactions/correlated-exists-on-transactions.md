## Problem Statement

Scenario: The compliance team needs to audit account activity. They want a list of accounts that have at least one transaction on record, accounts with no transactions at all, and &mdash; for risk review &mdash; accounts where at least one transaction exceeds that account&#39;s own average transaction amount.

Table: transactions

+------------+----------------------+
| Column | Type |
+------------+----------------------+
| txn_id | INT (PK) |

| txn_type | VARCHAR(10) |
| txn_date | DATE |
+------------+----------------------+

Tasks:

1. Retrieve the account_holder, branch, and balance of all Active accounts that have at least one transaction whose amount is greater than the average transaction amount for that same account. Use a correlated EXISTS subquery and a correlated aggregate subquery to calculate the account-specific average transaction amount.

### Output

----------------+-----------+-----------

(3 rows)

## Solution

```js
SELECT 
    account_holder, 
    branch, 
    balance
FROM 
    accounts a
WHERE 
    account_status = 'Active'
    AND EXISTS (
        SELECT 1
        FROM transactions t1
        WHERE t1.account_id = a.account_id
          AND t1.txn_amount > (
              SELECT AVG(t2.txn_amount)
              FROM transactions t2
              WHERE t2.account_id = a.account_id
          )
    );
```
